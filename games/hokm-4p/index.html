❓ How to Play

  1. Four seats: North, East, South (you), West. North+South are partners; East+West are partners.
  2. Each player is dealt 13 cards.
  3. The hakem (ruler) picks the trump suit for the round.
  4. The hakem leads the first trick. Play passes clockwise: North → East → South → West.
  5. Follow the led suit if you can — otherwise play anything, including trump.
  6. Highest trump wins the trick; with no trump played, highest card of the led suit wins.
  7. Whoever wins a trick leads the next one.
  8. Tricks are counted by team. First team to win 7 of the 13 tricks wins the round.
  9. The hakem role rotates clockwise each new round.
