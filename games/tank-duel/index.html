❓ How to Play

  1. Set your cannon's angle and power with the sliders.
  2. Watch the dotted preview line to line up your shot.
  3. Mind the wind — it pushes your shell left or right in flight.
  4. Tap "Fire!" to launch. Direct hits and near misses both do damage.
  5. The terrain gets blasted apart, so craters can help or hurt your next shot.
  6. First tank to lose all its health loses the round.
