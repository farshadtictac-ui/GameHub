❓ How to Play

  1. Each player is dealt 4 cards. The hakem (ruler) picks the trump suit from among their 4 cards.
  2. The hakem discards 2 of their 4 cards. Then the other player discards 2 of their own 4.
  3. Starting with the hakem, players take turns drawing from the shared deck one card at a time.
  4. You may take that card or discard it. Whatever you decide, the very next card is forced: if you took the first, you must discard the second — if you discarded the first, you must take the second.
  5. This continues, alternating turns, until the deck runs out — each player ends up with 13 cards.
  6. The hakem leads the first trick. Follow the led suit if you can; otherwise play anything, including trump.
  7. Highest trump wins the trick; if no trump is played, highest card of the led suit wins. Winner leads next.
  8. First to win 7 of the 13 tricks wins the round — and becomes hakem again next round.
