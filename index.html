❓ How to Play

  1. Place your soldiers inside your own zone — choose Heavy (3 hits), Medium (2 hits), or Light (1 hit).
  2. Tap "Confirm & Continue" once you're happy with your lineup.
  3. On your turn, drag the yellow ball backward to aim, then release to throw.
  4. Knock down all of your opponent's soldiers to win the round.
  5. Every 3 hits earns you a random power-up for your next throw.
